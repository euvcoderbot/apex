How to read these measurements
Qualifying pace
Fastest driver per team, against the fastest lap in the same Q phase. Official classification time must match a clean, dry timing lap. Q1 gives the widest field coverage. Missing or wet-only teams are not assigned a deficit.
Race pace
Dry, accurate green-flag laps, excluding pit laps and the opening two laps. Both timing-line gaps must exceed your threshold. This is a traffic proxy, not proof of an entire clean-air lap. Match compound, tyre age ±1 lap and race lap ±1. Fit team pace from median pairwise log-time differences, requiring five distinct laps on both sides of each pair and a connected group of at least three teams. Disconnected teams are excluded. No fuel correction. Low-coverage races, including Monaco, remain low-confidence or unavailable.
Cornering
Observed time and mean speed within up to 100 m either side of each circuit reference marker, shortened at adjacent midpoints. FastF1’s marker positions are manually curated, not precision surveyed. Categories use the median minimum speed of the loaded teams: low <150, medium 150–230, high ≥230 km/h. Compare identical corners; missing geometry is excluded. This is a lap-level observation, not a driver-independent car rating.
Straight line and braking
Top speed and full-throttle 95th-percentile speed are influenced by tow, deployment and DRS/straight mode. Brake-on zones show distance, entry/exit speed and mean deceleration, not brake pressure. Public sample rates cannot resolve tiny braking-point differences. There is no “best brakes” ranking from unmatched zones.
Tyre trend
Robust lap-time slope within each clean-air dry stint, with at least six laps and a five-lap age span. Fuel burn, track evolution and management remain confounders: this is observed s/lap, not isolated tyre degradation.
Season and development
Equal event weighting; never average seconds from different circuits. Coverage is shown per team. Changes in track mix, drivers and weather can shift the trend. A falling qualifying deficit is improvement relative to that event's leader, not proof of an upgrade benefit.
Downforce
Not estimated. Public telemetry lacks the tyre, ride-height, wind, mass and aero-load inputs needed for a defensible downforce index. High-speed corner performance is shown directly instead.
Reliability and results
Official finishing status separates known mechanical failures, incidents and other/unclassified retirements. Points and average finishing position describe results, not car speed. Unclassified causes are never assumed mechanical.

Sources: FastF1 timing and telemetry · FIA event documents. Data is fetched on demand; no performance results are saved between visits.
